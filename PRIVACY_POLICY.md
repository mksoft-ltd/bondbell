# Bondbell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Bondbell is an app for iPhone, iPad and Android that checks your Premium Bond
numbers against the prize lists NS&I publishes. It is made by Froggy Eye Ltd,
a company registered in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). This policy explains
what happens to information when you use Bondbell.

## In short

- Bondbell has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting
  and no tracking. We run no server for the app.
- Everything you enter stays on your device. We never receive your bond
  numbers, your holders' names or your results.
- When you ask Bondbell to check, your device connects directly to NS&I's
  website, nsandi.com, to download NS&I's public lists. Your bond numbers are
  not sent to NS&I; the comparison happens on your device.
- Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Reminders are scheduled on your
  device.

## What stays on your device

Bondbell saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

- the holder names you type (for example "Mum" or your own name);
- the bond numbers and ranges you save;
- your results: which of your bonds appeared on a list, the prize value, and
  when you checked (Bondbell keeps only matches, never NS&I's lists);
- your settings (such as reminders and sample mode) and whether you have
  bought Bondbell Pro.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Your holder names, bond numbers and results are kept together in one file in a
private folder inside the app. Each time Bondbell opens that file successfully
it keeps a recovery copy of it in the same folder, so your bonds can be
restored if the file is ever damaged; if a damaged file is found, it is set
aside in that folder rather than overwritten. These copies never leave the
device and are covered by the same backup rules below.

Backups work like this:

- On iPhone and iPad, your holder names, bond numbers and results are kept in
  a private folder inside the app that Bondbell marks as excluded from iCloud
  and computer backups, so they are not copied into any backup. Your settings
  and the record that you own Bondbell Pro are kept in the app's standard
  settings storage, which iOS can include in your own iCloud or computer
  backup; they contain no bond numbers, names or results. We have no access to
  your backups.
- On Android, Bondbell switches off Android's cloud backup and device-to-device
  transfer for all of its data, so nothing it saves is backed up or
  transferred.

Because your bonds are not backed up, a new or reset device starts empty: you
would add your bond numbers again. A Pro purchase can always be restored from
the store.

You can delete every holder, bond number and saved result at any time in
Settings, using "Delete all my data". This includes sample-mode data and the
recovery and damaged copies described above. Your settings, your reminders and
your Bondbell Pro purchase are kept. Removing the app removes all of its data
from the device.

## The connection to NS&I

Bondbell connects to NS&I only when you ask it to: when you check a draw,
show the available draws, or search the unclaimed-prizes list. Your device
then connects directly to https://www.nsandi.com to read NS&I's downloads
page and download the list file. Nothing passes through a server of ours.
The app's only other connections are to Apple's or Google's purchase system,
described under "Purchases" below.

As with visiting any website, NS&I's servers receive your device's IP address
and the details of the request, including which file was asked for and a
user agent that names the app, its version and your device's platform (for
example "Bondbell/1.0.0 (iOS; personal bond checker;
+https://bondbell.froggyeye.com)"). Your bond numbers, holder names and
results are never sent. Bondbell does not keep cookies from NS&I's website.
How NS&I handles that request is covered by NS&I's own privacy notice.

The downloaded list is read on your device and deleted as soon as the check
finishes, or when you cancel it.

Bondbell is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
connected to NS&I (National Savings and Investments).

## Purchases

Bondbell Pro and the optional tips are bought through Apple's App Store or
Google Play. The store handles payment and holds your purchase record under
its own privacy policy; we never see your card details. When the app starts,
it asks the store for product prices, and on Android it also asks Google Play
for any earlier purchases so that Pro can be restored. On Android, Google's
Play Billing library, which handles purchases, also sends Google its own
technical information about how purchases are working; that is handled by
Google under Google's privacy policy, and it does not include your bond
numbers, holder names or results. We receive only the sales and payment
reports that Apple and Google give to developers.

## Notifications

If you turn on the results-day reminder, Bondbell asks for permission to show
notifications and schedules the reminders on your device. No push service or
server is involved, and you can turn the reminder off at any time in Settings.

## Links to other websites

Some buttons open a web page in your browser, such as NS&I's pages, our
support page at https://bondbell.froggyeye.com or this policy. Those websites
have their own privacy policies.

## If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use
it only to reply, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

## Children

Bondbell is made for adults and is not directed at children. The app collects
no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because the app sends us no personal data, we hold none about you from your
use of Bondbell. Under UK data protection law you can ask us what information
we hold about you, and you have the right to complain to the Information
Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes to this policy

If the way Bondbell handles information changes, we will update this policy
and its effective date before the change reaches the app.

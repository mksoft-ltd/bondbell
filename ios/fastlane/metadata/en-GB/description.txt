Check your Premium Bond numbers against each month's prize draw without logging in to anything. Save your bond numbers once, and whenever you tap Check, Bondbell compares them with NS&I's published list of winning numbers, right here on your iPhone or iPad.

NO LOGIN, NO ACCOUNT
There is nothing to sign up for. Your bond numbers are saved on your device and are never sent anywhere. When you check, Bondbell downloads NS&I's public list directly from nsandi.com, compares it with your numbers on the device, keeps only the matches and deletes the download.

OLD PAPER CERTIFICATES AND FAMILY BONDS
Add bonds as ranges or single numbers, from a statement or from old paper certificates. It works just as well for a parent's or grandparent's bonds as for your own. If a number can't be read, Bondbell explains why in plain English, for example when a holder's number has been typed instead of a bond number.

CHECK THE LATEST DRAW
See the size of the file before it downloads, follow the progress, and cancel at any time. The result lists any prizes by holder, or says plainly that none of your bonds is on that month's list. Every result reminds you to confirm with NS&I, and when prizes are listed it explains how to claim them.

UNCLAIMED PRIZES, BACK TO 1957
Search NS&I's list of prizes that were never claimed, which goes back to 1957. The list shows the value of each unclaimed prize, and claims are made through NS&I.

RESULTS-DAY REMINDER
Turn on an optional reminder at 9am UK time on the day new results are expected. It tells you the new list should be available; it does not tell you whether you have a prize.

TRY IT FIRST
"Try with sample numbers" runs complete checks with made-up numbers, so you can see how it works before adding your own. In sample mode every Pro feature is open to try on the sample data. Sample data is clearly labelled and kept apart from your real bonds.

BONDBELL PRO
One purchase, yours for good. No subscription. Pro adds:
• Unlimited holders (the free version covers one holder, with as many ranges as you like)
• Earlier draws: check any of the months NS&I currently publishes, usually about six
• Unclaimed search for all your saved bonds at once (free searches one number at a time)
• History and stats for every draw you have checked

Checking the latest draw, single-number unclaimed searches, reminders and sample mode are all free.

TIP JAR
If Bondbell is useful to you, you can leave an optional tip. Tips are a thank-you only and unlock nothing.

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no server of ours in the middle. Purchases are handled by Apple. Your bond numbers are not included in device backups, and you can delete them and your results from Settings at any time.

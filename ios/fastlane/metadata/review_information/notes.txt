WHAT THE APP DOES
Bondbell lets UK savers check their own Premium Bond numbers against the monthly prize draw results. The user saves bond number ranges (from a statement or old paper certificates). When they tap to check, the app downloads NS&I's publicly published winning-numbers file directly from www.nsandi.com, compares it with the saved numbers on the device, keeps only the matches and deletes the download. It also searches NS&I's public list of unclaimed prizes and can schedule an optional local reminder on the day new results are expected.

NO LOGIN; HOW TO TRY EVERYTHING, INCLUDING PRO, WITHOUT BUYING
There are no accounts. Sample mode opens every Pro feature on built-in, made-up sample data (two sample holders, three sample draws, two sample unclaimed prizes). From a fresh install:
1. Onboarding: Next, Next, "Try with sample numbers". (Or later: "Try with sample numbers" on the Check tab, or Settings > "Sample mode".)
2. Latest draw: Check tab > "Check the latest draw" > "Check the sample". Result: 2 sample prizes worth £125.
3. Earlier draws (Pro): Check tab > "Earlier draws" card > "October 2026 (sample)" > "Check the sample".
4. Unclaimed search for all bonds (Pro): Unclaimed tab > "Search 20,100 bonds" > "Check the sample".
5. History and stats (Pro): History tab.
6. More than one holder (Pro): My bonds > "Add holder".
A banner says "Sample mode: made-up numbers, not real results" and that Pro features are open to try with sample data. Its "Exit" returns to the user's real data, where Pro features stay locked until purchased. Sample data never mixes with real data.

PURCHASES
- Bondbell Pro (com.froggyeye.bondbell.pro, non-consumable): unlimited holders, earlier draws, unclaimed search for all saved bonds at once, history and stats. Settings > "See what Pro adds" shows the real buy button and price (in sample mode too). "Restore purchases" is on the Pro screen and in Settings.
- Tips (com.froggyeye.bondbell.tip.small / .medium / .large, consumable): optional thank-you payments that unlock nothing, as the app says next to them. They are in the Tip jar card in Settings, and as one quiet card under a real result that lists prizes (never in sample mode, never on a no-prize result).

NOT A FINANCIAL SERVICE, NOT GAMBLING
- Bondbell reads only the lists NS&I publishes for anyone to download, on the user's device and at the user's request. It offers no NS&I account access, no login to NS&I, no buying or selling of bonds, no payments, no transfers and no financial advice.
- Premium Bonds are a UK government savings product sold by NS&I. Holders keep their capital and the monthly prize draw takes the place of interest. Bondbell sells no entries, takes no stakes and pays no prizes; it only checks published results. Every result tells the user to confirm with NS&I.

PERMISSIONS
Notifications only, requested when the user turns on the results-day reminder, never at launch. Reminders are scheduled locally; there is no push service.

PRIVACY
No accounts, analytics, ads, tracking or server of ours. The app's own network requests go only to www.nsandi.com when the user asks for a check (NS&I sees the device's IP address and the app's user agent, never bond numbers); purchases go through StoreKit. Bond data is stored in an app folder excluded from backups. Privacy label: Data Not Collected.

INDEPENDENCE
Bondbell is an independent app by Froggy Eye Ltd, not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to NS&I, and says so in onboarding, on the Check tab, in Settings and in About. "Premium Bonds" is used only to describe which product's results the app checks.

REAL DATA
A real check downloads about 34 MB from nsandi.com (the size is shown first). Any valid range can be entered, e.g. 100AB100000 to 100AB100999.
